# Roadmap

One version per research phase. Month 1 is November 2026. Dates are planning targets, not guarantees.

| Phase | Months | Dates | Version | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation, data, baselines | 1-3 | 11/2026 – 02/2027 | v0.1 | Public datasets, success definition, reproducible benchmark and baseline results |
| 2. Laboratory-constratint feasibility assessment and outcome logging | 3-8 | 02/2027 – 06/2027 | v0.2 | Inventory and equipment assessment module; outcome-logging schema and library |
| 3. AiZynthFinder integration | 8-12 | 06/2027 – 11/2027 | v0.3 | Custom-scorer integration; comparison against the stock-aware baseline |
| 4. Laboratory-adaptive success estimates | 12-20 | 11/2027 – 06/2028 | v0.4 | Calibrated success and uncertainty estimates, out-of-distribution detection, adaptation to local outcomes |
| 5. Comparative evaluation, release, dissemination | 20-24 | 06/2028 – 10/2028 | v1.0 | Final comparison, documentation, preprint; prospective validation where a laboratory partner exists |

Current code is a pre-release scaffold (v0.0.x): early work toward Phases 2 and 3. Reserve v0.1 for a finished Phase 1.

## Evidence tiers

1. Simulated constraints (synthetic inventories and equipment profiles)
2. Historical experimental results from public datasets
3. Prospective laboratory validation
